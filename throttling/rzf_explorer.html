Randomized zero forcing · throttling

Where set size stops
paying for speed.

In randomized zero forcing a white vertex turns blue with probability equal to the fraction of its in-neighbours that are already blue. The throttling number asks how to spend a budget: every vertex you start blue costs one, and every round you wait costs one. Build a starting set on any family below, measure its expected propagation time, and solve for the exact optimum.

thrzf(G)  =  minS ⊆ V(G) ( |S|  +  eptrzf(G, S) )
